Graduate Machine Learning Engineer

About the role
We're a London-based FinTech building tools that turn financial data into decisions.
You'll join a small, fast-moving team and own real machine learning work from day one,
from prototyping models to shipping them into our products.

What you'll do
- Build and evaluate machine learning and NLP models on financial and text data
- Write clean, tested Python and help take models from notebook to production
- Work with SQL databases to pull, clean, and analyse data
- Collaborate closely with engineers and product on what to build next

Required
- Strong programming ability in Python
- Solid grounding in machine learning fundamentals
- Experience with NLP or working with text data
- Comfortable with SQL and data analysis
- A degree in Computer Science, AI, or a related field

Preferred
- Hands-on experience with PyTorch or another deep learning framework
- Exposure to large language models (LLMs) or transformers
- Familiarity with Git and collaborative development

Nice to have
- Experience with Docker or a cloud platform (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- An interest in financial markets

We care about people who ship, take ownership, and learn fast. No specific years of
experience required — strong graduates and self-taught builders are very welcome.
